## Local Listings Management

### Understanding Local Citations

Local citations are online mentions of your business's Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP). They serve as trust signals to search engines and directly impact local search rankings.

**Types of Citations:**

1. **Structured Citations:** Business directories with specific fields
   - Google Business Profile
   - Yelp, Yellow Pages
   - Industry-specific directories

2. **Unstructured Citations:** Mentions on websites without specific format
   - Blog posts
   - News articles
   - Social media mentions

1. **Proximity** (25% weight)
   - Physical distance to searcher
   - Service area coverage
   
2. **Relevance** (35% weight)
   - Business category accuracy
   - Keyword usage in business description
   - Services list completeness
   - Attributes and amenities
   
3. **Prominence** (40% weight)
   - Review quantity and quality
   - Citation consistency and quantity
   - Website authority

```

• Take conversation offline
• Follow up privately

"Jessica, we're genuinely sorry about your experience

to make this right. Could you please contact me directly

Sincerely,
David Wilson
General Manager"

□ Follow up again in 1 week
□ Some customers will update their review after resolution
```

**2. Review Generation Ethics**
```
✅ ALLOWED (Google-Compliant):
• Ask all customers for reviews
• Send follow-up requests post-service
• Remind customers about reviews
• Make it easy to leave reviews
• Incentivize reviews indirectly (e.g., enter all 
  reviewers in monthly drawing)
• Display review badges on website
• Thank customers who leave reviews

❌ PROHIBITED (Policy Violations):
• Offer discounts/gifts ONLY for 5-star reviews
• Write fake reviews
• Pay for reviews
• Review gating (filtering based on rating)
• Threatening negative reviewers
• Using multiple accounts to boost reviews
• Soliciting reviews in bulk from non-customers
• Removing all negative reviews (attempted review fraud)

Ethical Framework:
"Ask everyone, make it easy, never filter"

Your request should be neutral:
Good: "We'd love your feedback on your experience"
Bad: "If you were happy, please leave us a review"

Result: Authentic reviews, compliant with platforms
```
