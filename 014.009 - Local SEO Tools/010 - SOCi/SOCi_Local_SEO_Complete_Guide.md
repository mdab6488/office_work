## Overview

### What is SOCi?

SOCi (Social Channel Intelligence) is an enterprise-grade **CoMarketing Cloud platform** designed specifically for multi-location businesses to automate and scale local marketing across search, social media, and reputation management channels. The platform serves nearly 1,000 brands including Ford, Ace Hardware, Jersey Mike's, Kumon, Liberty Tax, and Anytime Fitness.

**Core Value Proposition:**
- Manages local marketing at scale without adding headcount
- Automates the work of "1,000 local marketers" through Genius AI
- Centralizes search, social, and reputation in one platform
- Maintains brand consistency while enabling local customization

### Target Users
- Multi-location franchises (10+ locations)
- Enterprise retail chains
- Hospitality and restaurant groups
- Healthcare networks
- Property management companies
- Service-based franchises (gyms, salons, automotive)

### Platform Foundation
- **Founded:** 2012

- **Technology:** Proprietary Genius AI™ with suite of Genius Agents™
- **Scale:** Manages thousands of locations per client
- **Integrations:** 50+ platforms including Google, Meta, Yelp, Apple Maps, TikTok, LinkedIn

## Genius AI Agents

SOCi's latest innovation (Fall 2025) introduces **Genius Agents** - the first brand-trained digital workforce for local marketing. These agents work 24/7 across every location, independently managing workflows.

### Genius Search Agent

**Primary Function:** Drives local SEO by analyzing signals, optimizing listings and pages, and publishing localized content.

**Core Capabilities:**
- **Automated Listings Management:** Maintains accurate NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across 50+ directories
- **Real-Time Optimization:** Analyzes local search patterns and automatically implements improvements
- **Google Posts Automation:** Creates and publishes location-specific Google Business Profile posts
- **Category Optimization:** Identifies and adds relevant service categories based on search trends
- **Keyword Intelligence:** Monitors local search queries and adjusts listings accordingly
- **Duplicate Detection:** Weekly scans to identify and flag duplicate listings
- **Apple Maps Integration:** Automatically maps Google Place Page URLs to Apple Action buttons

**How It Works:**
1. **Training Phase:** You teach the agent your brand voice, workflows, and priorities through chat
2. **Analysis Phase:** Agent analyzes real-time data from:
   - Google Search Console
   - Google Business Profile insights
   - Competitor rankings
   - Local search trends
   - Review mentions
   - Social engagement data
3. **Recommendation Phase:** Generates hundreds of location-specific optimization tasks
4. **Execution Phase:** Implements approved changes with one click or automatically based on your rules
5. **Monitoring Phase:** Tracks impact on rankings, traffic, and engagement

**Control Settings:**
- **Full Automation:** Agent publishes all changes automatically
- **Hybrid Mode:** Auto-publish low-risk changes, route high-impact for approval
- **Manual Review:** All changes require human approval

## Best Practices

### 1. Listings Management Best Practices

**Data Accuracy is Paramount:**
```
NAP Consistency Rules:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
❌ Wrong: "McDonald's #4172"
✅ Right: "McDonald's"

❌ Wrong: "123 Main Street Suite 4"
✅ Right: "123 Main St Ste 4" (match exactly everywhere)

❌ Wrong: "(555) 123-4567"
✅ Right: "5551234567" or "555-123-4567" (be consistent)

❌ Wrong: "Open Daily"
✅ Right: Specific hours for each day

Key Principle: Once you choose a format, use it EVERYWHERE
```

**Category Selection Strategy:**
```
Primary Category:
- Choose the ONE most accurate category
- This is most important for search rankings
- Cannot be changed frequently (Google penalties)

Secondary Categories:
- Add all relevant categories (up to 9 additional)
- Include both broad and specific
- Update quarterly based on search trends

Example (Restaurant):
Primary: "Italian Restaurant"
Secondary: "Pizza Restaurant," "Wine Bar," "Catering," 
          "Takeout Restaurant," "Gluten-Free Restaurant"

Why: Captures searches for "Italian food," "pizza near me," 
     "gluten free dining," etc.
```

**Photo Best Practices:**
```
Photo Strategy:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Minimum Requirements:
- Logo: 1 high-res logo image
- Exterior: 3-5 different angles/times of day
- Interior: 10-15 images (dining area, bar, kitchen, restrooms)
- Products: 20-30 images (menu items, products)
- Team: 5-10 images (staff, chef, manager)
- Common areas: 5+ images (parking, entrance, signage)

Upload Frequency:
- New photos monthly (Google prioritizes fresh content)
- Seasonal updates (holiday decorations, summer patio)
- Event photos (community involvement)

Image Specs:
- Resolution: 720px minimum, 1920px ideal
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File size: <5MB
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 for best display
```

**Google Posts Frequency:**
```
Recommended Posting Schedule:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Weekly: 2-3 posts minimum
Ideal: 5-7 posts (daily)

Post Types Mix:
- 40% Product/Service highlights
- 30% Offers/Promotions
- 20% Events
- 10% Updates/News

Why Frequent Posts Matter:
- Posts expire after 7 days
- Fresh content signals active business
- More posts = more search visibility
- Captures different search intents

### 2. Review Management Best Practices

**Response Time Importance:**
```
Impact of Response Speed:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
<1 hour:    Exceptional (builds trust)
1-4 hours:  Great (shows attentiveness)
4-24 hours: Good (acceptable)
1-3 days:   Poor (customer may disengage)
>3 days:    Very Poor (significant trust damage)

Research Shows:
- 53% of customers expect response within 7 days
- Businesses that respond are seen as 1.7X more trustworthy
- Response time impacts future review volume
```

**Response Best Practices:**

**5-Star Reviews:**
```
Template Structure:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Thank the reviewer by name
2. Acknowledge specific details they mentioned
3. Reinforce your brand value
4. Invite them back

Example:
"Thank you so much for the 5-star review, Jennifer! We're 
thrilled you enjoyed our new seasonal menu, especially the 
butternut squash ravioli. Our chef works hard to source local 
ingredients, and it means the world to hear you appreciated 
it. We look forward to serving you again soon!"

Why This Works:
✓ Personal (uses name, specific dish)
✓ Highlights brand value (local ingredients)
✓ Invites return visit
✓ Other potential customers see engaged business
```

**1-2 Star Reviews:**
```
Template Structure:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Apologize sincerely
2. Acknowledge their specific issue (don't defend)
3. Explain corrective action (without overpromising)
4. Invite offline resolution with direct contact
5. Thank them for feedback

Example:
"We're very sorry to hear about your experience, Michael. 
Your wait time of 45 minutes is far below our standard, and 
that's on us. We've discussed this with our team and are 
implementing new processes to prevent this. Please contact 
our general manager directly at sarah@restaurant.com so we 
can make this right. Thank you for bringing this to our 
attention—it helps us improve."

Why This Works:
✓ Takes responsibility
✓ Shows action being taken
✓ Moves conversation offline (protects reputation)
✓ Demonstrates commitment to improvement

⚠️ Avoid:
✗ Making excuses
✗ Blaming the customer
✗ Overly defensive tone
✗ Ignoring the review
```

**Review Generation Strategy:**
```
When to Ask for Reviews:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Optimal Timing:
- 2-24 hours after positive interaction
- Post-purchase (for retail)

### 3. Review Generation at Scale

**Systematic Review Generation:**
```
Multi-Channel Review Request Strategy:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Channel 1: Email (Highest Response Rate)
- Send 2-24 hours after positive interaction
- Subject: "How was your visit to [Location Name]?"
- Body: Short, personal, direct link to review
- Expected response rate: 5-15%

Channel 2: SMS (Fast Response)
- Send within 1 hour of service completion
- Keep very short: "Hi [Name], thanks for visiting! If you 
  enjoyed your experience, we'd love a review: [link]"
- Expected response rate: 3-8%

Channel 3: In-Person (Highest Quality)
- Train staff to ask satisfied customers
- Provide QR code cards at checkout
- Script: "We'd love to hear about your experience. Scan 
          this code to leave a quick review!"
- Expected response rate: 15-25%

Channel 4: On-Site Signage
- Place QR codes on receipts, table tents, exit doors
- Expected response rate: 1-3%

Combined Strategy:
- Use all channels
- 500 monthly customers
- 50 ask in-person (15% = 7-8 reviews)
- 200 get email (10% = 20 reviews)
- 100 get SMS (5% = 5 reviews)
- Total: 32-33 new reviews monthly
```

**Timing Optimization:**
```
When to Ask for Reviews:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Optimal Times:
✓ Immediately after positive interaction (in-person)
✓ 2-4 hours post-purchase (email/SMS for retail)
✓ 24 hours post-service (for services)
✓ After problem resolution (shows you fixed it)

Poor Times:
✗ During the service/purchase (interrupts experience)
✗ Weeks later (too late, customer forgot)
✗ After a known negative experience (obvious)

Industry-Specific Timing:
- Restaurant: Same day or next morning
- Auto Service: Immediately after pickup
- Healthcare: 24-48 hours after visit
- Retail: Same day
- Hotels: Day of checkout or next day
```

**Review Funneling Strategy:**
```
Smart Review Distribution:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Problem: You want reviews on Google (most important), but 
         customers may prefer other platforms.

Solution: Let satisfied customers choose, route unhappy 
          customers to private feedback.

Implementation:
1. Initial Survey/Question:
   "How was your experience? ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐"

2. If 5-Star Response:
   "Great! Would you mind sharing on Google?"
   → Link to Google review

3. If 4-Star Response:
   "Thanks! We'd love to improve. Where should we focus?"
   → Collect specific feedback
   → Optional review link

4. If 1-3 Star Response:
   "We're sorry we fell short. Can you share details so 
    we can fix this?"
   → Route to internal feedback form
   → No review link (avoid public negative reviews)
   → Proactive outreach to resolve

This maximizes positive reviews, minimizes negative reviews, 
and captures improvement feedback.
```
