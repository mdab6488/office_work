## Overview

LocaliQ (formerly known as ReachLocal) is an all-in-one digital marketing platform specifically designed to help small and medium-sized businesses improve their local search presence and attract nearby customers. It combines local SEO, paid advertising, social media management, and marketing automation into a single platform with a focus on driving local leads and revenue.

### Primary Use Case
LocaliQ is ideal for businesses that rely on local customers, such as:
- Restaurants and cafes
- Medical and dental practices
- Law firms and professional services
- Home service providers (plumbers, electricians, HVAC)
- Retail stores
- Automotive services
- Real estate agencies

### 4. Review Velocity Optimization

#### Strategic Review Acquisition

**The Review Growth Formula:**

```
Target Monthly Reviews = (Competitor Average × 1.5) or Minimum 20 reviews/month

If you have 50 reviews and competitor average is 150:
Monthly target = 150 × 1.5 = 225 total / 12 months = ~19 new reviews/month
```

## Common Pitfalls

### 1. NAP Inconsistencies

**Problem:**
Business listed with variations across different platforms:
- "Main St" vs "Main Street"
- "Suite 100" vs "#100" vs "Ste 100"
- "(555) 123-4567" vs "555-123-4567" vs "555.123.4567"

**Impact:**
- Confuses Google's algorithms
- Reduces local ranking potential
- Lost customer calls/visits

**Solution Using LocaliQ:**
1. Run listing audit to identify all variations
2. Choose ONE standard format
3. Use bulk update feature to fix all at once
4. Monitor for new inconsistencies
5. Suppress duplicate listings

**Prevention:**
- Document official NAP format
- Train all staff on official format
- Regular quarterly audits
- Lock listings where possible

---

### 2. Review Gating (Illegal Practice)

**Problem:**
Only requesting reviews from happy customers (filtering out unhappy customers before they can review)

**Why It's Wrong:**
- Violates Google's policies
- Can result in GBP suspension
- Illegal in some jurisdictions
- Damages trust if discovered

**Legal Approach:**
- Request reviews from ALL customers
- Address issues before they become reviews
- Respond professionally to negative reviews
- Follow up to resolve issues (may lead to updated review)

---

### 3. Keyword Stuffing

**Problem:**
Forcing too many keywords unnaturally into business description, posts, or reviews

**Bad Example:**
```
"Phoenix plumber plumbing Phoenix AZ emergency plumber Phoenix 
best plumber Phoenix cheap plumber Phoenix 24 hour plumber Phoenix"
```

**Good Example:**
```
"Since 2010, we've provided reliable emergency plumbing services 
throughout Phoenix and surrounding areas. Our licensed plumbers 
handle everything from simple repairs to complex installations."
```

**Guidelines:**
- Primary keyword: 2-4 times in 750-word description
- Secondary keywords: 1-2 times each
- Keywords should flow naturally
- Focus on user readability first

---

### 4. Ignoring Negative Reviews

**Problem:**
Not responding to negative reviews (or responding defensively/argumentatively)

**Impact:**
- Shows potential customers you don't care
- Negative reviews stay top of mind
- Missed opportunity to demonstrate customer service
- Can lead to more negative reviews

**Correct Approach:**
1. Respond within 6 hours
2. Apologize genuinely (even if you disagree)
3. Take conversation offline
4. Offer specific solution
5. Follow up to resolve
6. Request review update if resolved

---

### 5. Inconsistent Posting

**Problem:**
Posting frequently for 2 weeks, then nothing for 2 months

**Impact:**
- Reduces visibility in local search
- Appears inactive to potential customers
- Loses momentum in rankings

**Solution:**
- Create content calendar in LocaliQ
- Schedule posts 2-4 weeks in advance
- Set reminders for content creation
- Repurpose successful posts
- Use LocaliQ's content suggestions

**Minimum Frequency:**
- B2C services: 2-3 times per week
- B2B services: 1-2 times per week
- Restaurants/retail: 3-5 times per week
- Seasonal businesses: Daily during peak season

---

### 6. Neglecting Photo Updates

**Problem:**
Same photos for 2+ years, outdated images, poor quality photos

**Impact:**
- Lower engagement on GBP
- Appears outdated or closed
- Competitors with fresh content rank higher
- Lost opportunities to showcase new offerings

**Solution:**
- Quarterly photo refresh (25% of photos)
- Annual complete photo audit
- Add photos after renovations/updates
- Seasonal photo updates
- Remove dated photos (old promotions, former employees)

---

### 7. Category Mistakes

**Common Errors:**
- Choosing overly broad category (e.g., "Business" instead of "Plumber")
- Picking incorrect category for better visibility
- Not using all available secondary categories
- Choosing categories for keywords rather than services

**Impact:**
- Shows in irrelevant searches

- Confuses potential customers
- Wastes local SEO potential
