
## ROI Measurement

### Key Metrics to Track

**Traffic Metrics:**
- Organic sessions
- Organic pageviews
- New vs. returning visitors
- Geographic distribution

**Ranking Metrics:**
- Keywords in top 3
- Keywords in top 10
- Average position
- Share of voice
- Featured snippets owned

**Conversion Metrics:**
- Organic conversion rate
- Lead volume from organic
- Revenue attributed to organic
- Customer acquisition cost

**Efficiency Metrics:**
- Time saved on reporting
- Issues identified per week
- Opportunities captured
- Team productivity improvements

### ROI Calculation Example

```
Enterprise E-commerce Company

Annual Platform Investment:
- BrightEdge license: $120,000
- Implementation: $30,000
- Training: $15,000
- Ongoing support: $20,000
Total Investment: $185,000

Results After 12 Months:
- Organic traffic increase: +47%
- Additional sessions: 847,000/month
- Average order value: $147
- Conversion rate: 2.3%
- Additional monthly revenue: $2,863,000
- Additional annual revenue: $34,356,000

ROI Calculation:
- Revenue: $34,356,000
- Investment: $185,000
- ROI: 18,465%
- Payback period: 4.6 days

Efficiency Gains:
- SEO team time saved: 480 hours/month
- Time saved value: $96,000/year (at $200/hour)
- Faster issue resolution: $340,000 losses prevented
- Better prioritization: $4.2M opportunity capture

Total Value Created: $38.9M
```
