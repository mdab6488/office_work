
## BrightEdge Platform

### Overview

**BrightEdge** is the leading enterprise SEO and content performance marketing platform, serving companies like Microsoft, Adobe, and 3M.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Data Cube technology for real-time insights
- Content IQ for AI-driven recommendations
- Strong predictive analytics
- Extensive integration capabilities

- Technical SEO monitoring
