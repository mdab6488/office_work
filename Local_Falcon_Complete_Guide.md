## What is Local Falcon

Local Falcon is a specialized **local SEO rank tracking tool** that helps businesses monitor their Google Business Profile (GBP) visibility across precise geographic areas. Unlike traditional rank trackers that show positions from a single location, Local Falcon uses a unique "grid scan" technology to visualize exactly where your business appears in the Local Pack and organic results across multiple GPS coordinates.

### Why Local Falcon Exists

**The Problem:**
- Google's local search results vary dramatically based on the searcher's exact location
- Traditional rank trackers only check from one central point
- Businesses need to understand their visibility across their entire service area
- Competitors might dominate certain neighborhoods while you dominate others

**The Solution:**
Local Falcon scans a geographic grid around your business location, checking rankings from dozens or hundreds of points simultaneously, creating a heat map of your local search visibility.

## Core Functionality

### 1. Grid Scan Technology

**How It Works:**
```
Scan Setup Process:
1. Enter your business name and location
2. Define scan radius (e.g., 5-mile radius)
3. Set grid density (number of scan points)
4. Choose keywords to track
5. Execute scan across all grid points
```

**What You Get:**
- Heat map visualization showing where you rank
- Pin colors indicating ranking position at each point
- Coverage percentage across the entire area
- Distance-based ranking decay patterns

**Example Scan Parameters:**
```
Business: "Joe's Pizza Brooklyn"
Center Point: 123 Main St, Brooklyn, NY
Radius: 3 miles
Grid Points: 81 (9x9 grid)
Keywords: "pizza near me", "pizza delivery", "best pizza"
```

### 2. Visual Heat Maps

Local Falcon displays results as colored pins on Google Maps:

- **Dark Green:** Rank #1 in Local 3-Pack
- **Light Green:** Rank #2 in Local 3-Pack  
- **Yellow:** Rank #3 in Local 3-Pack
- **Orange:** Rank #4-10 (visible in expanded results)
- **Red:** Rank #11-20
- **Gray:** Not ranking in top 20

**Real-Life Interpretation:**
If your Brooklyn pizza shop shows mostly green pins in a 1-mile radius but orange/red further out, you have strong hyper-local visibility but need to improve for broader searches.

## Grid Scan Technology

### Understanding Scan Points

**Grid Density Options:**

**Basic Scan (25 points):**
- 5x5 grid
- Fast results (1-2 minutes)
- Good for: Quick checks, daily monitoring
- Use when: You need speed over precision

**Standard Scan (81 points):**
- 9x9 grid
- Moderate speed (3-5 minutes)
- Good for: Regular audits, client reports
- Use when: Balance between detail and speed

**Dense Scan (225 points):**
- 15x15 grid
- Slower results (8-15 minutes)
- Good for: Detailed analysis, competitive research
- Use when: You need maximum precision

**Ultra-Dense Scan (625+ points):**
- 25x25 grid
- Longest processing (20-30 minutes)
- Good for: Major cities, critical campaigns
- Use when: Enterprise clients, high-stakes audits

### Scan Radius Strategy

**Radius Selection Guide:**

```
Business Type → Recommended Radius

Restaurants, Cafes: 1-3 miles
Retail Stores: 2-5 miles
Service Businesses (plumbers, electricians): 5-10 miles
Professional Services (lawyers, doctors): 3-7 miles
Regional Businesses (contractors): 10-25 miles
```

**Real Example - Radius Testing:**
```
Auto Repair Shop: "Fast Lane Auto"
Location: Suburban Chicago

Test 1: 2-mile radius
- Result: 95% top-3 coverage
- Insight: Strong local dominance

Test 2: 5-mile radius  
- Result: 58% top-3 coverage
- Insight: Weak in outer suburbs

Test 3: 10-mile radius
- Result: 22% top-3 coverage
- Insight: Virtually invisible in most service area

Decision: Focus optimization on 3-5 mile ring where most customers originate
```
