
## Introduction to WordPress SEO

WordPress powers over 43% of all websites globally, making it the most popular CMS platform. Its SEO-friendly architecture and extensive plugin ecosystem make it an excellent choice for SEO professionals.

### Why WordPress is SEO-Friendly

**Core SEO Advantages:**
- Clean, semantic HTML structure
- Customizable permalink structure
- Built-in RSS feeds
- Easy content management
- Mobile-responsive by default
- Fast development and deployment
- Extensive plugin ecosystem

**Real-World Example:**
A local bakery migrated from a custom HTML site to WordPress and saw a 156% increase in organic traffic within 6 months due to easier content updates, better internal linking, and mobile optimization.
