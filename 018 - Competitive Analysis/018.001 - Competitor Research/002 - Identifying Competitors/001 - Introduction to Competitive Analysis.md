
### Identifying Competitors

#### Types of Competitors

**1. Direct Search Competitors**
Websites that rank for your target keywords, regardless of whether they're business competitors.

**Example:**
```
Your Business: Organic coffee shop in Portland
Business Competitors: Other coffee shops in Portland
Search Competitors: 
- Starbucks.com (ranks for "best coffee Portland")
- Local food blogs (rank for "Portland coffee guide")
- Yelp listings
- Travel websites
```

**2. Business Competitors**
Companies that sell similar products/services but may not rank well organically.

**3. Aspirational Competitors**
Industry leaders you want to compete with eventually.

#### Method 1: Manual Google Search

**Step-by-Step Process:**

```
Step 1: List Your Core Keywords
- "project management software"
- "team collaboration tools"
- "task management app"

Step 2: Search Each Keyword in Incognito Mode
- Document top 10 results for each
- Note: Use incognito to avoid personalized results

Step 3: Create Competitor Matrix
Keyword                     | Position 1 | Position 2 | Position 3
---------------------------|------------|------------|------------
project management software| Asana.com  | Monday.com | Trello.com
team collaboration tools   | Slack.com  | Teams      | Asana.com

#### Method 2: Using SEO Tools

**A. Ahrefs Competitor Discovery**

```
Process:
1. Go to Ahrefs → Site Explorer
2. Enter your domain
3. Navigate to "Competing Domains"
4. Ahrefs shows domains competing for same keywords

Real Example Output:
Your Site: YourFitnessBlog.com
Top Competing Domains:
- Bodybuilding.com (1,247 common keywords)
- Menshealth.com (892 common keywords)
- VeryWellFit.com (756 common keywords)
- HealthLine.com (643 common keywords)
```

**B. SEMrush Competitor Research**

```
Steps:
1. SEMrush → Domain Overview
2. Enter your domain
3. Scroll to "Main Organic Competitors"

#### Method 3: Reverse Keyword Research

**Process:**
Find who ranks for your dream keywords before you do.

```
Real Example:
Goal: Rank for "best CRM software"

Step 1: Search Google for "best CRM software"
Step 2: List all ranking domains:
- Salesforce.com/blog
- HubSpot.com/crm
- Forbes.com/advisor/crm
- PCMag.com/reviews/crm
- Capterra.com/crm-software
- G2.com/categories/crm

Step 3: Analyze Common Factors:
✓ All have 2,000+ words
✓ All include comparison tables
✓ All have video content
✓ Average 15+ backlinks
✓ Domain Authority 70+
```
