**Method 2: Broken Link Building**

```
Finding Broken Links on Competitor Sites:

Competitor: MajorIndustryBlog.com
Tool: Ahrefs → Site Explorer → Best by Links → Filter: 404

Broken Links Found (High Priority):
URL (Now 404)                              | Backlinks | Referring Domains
-------------------------------------------|-----------|------------------
/ultimate-guide-content-marketing          | 47        | 31
/social-media-strategy-template            | 38        | 25
/free-seo-checklist-download              | 52        | 34

Outreach Strategy:
1. Create better version of broken content
2. Find who linked to broken page:
   - Use Ahrefs "Backlinks" report
   - Export linking domains
   
3. Outreach Template:
"Hi [Name],
I noticed you linked to [broken URL] on [their site], but 
that page is no longer available. I created a similar 
resource at [your URL] that might be a good replacement.
Would you consider updating the link?
Best,
[Your Name]"

Expected Success Rate: 15-25% response rate
Potential New Backlinks: 23 high-quality links
```
