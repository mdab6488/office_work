
## Creating a Competitive Analysis Report

### Report Structure

```markdown
# Competitive Analysis Report
## [Your Company Name]
Date: [Date]
Prepared by: [Your Name]

## Executive Summary
- Industry overview
- Key findings
- Top 3 recommendations
- Expected impact

## Competitor Identification
### Direct Competitors
1. [Competitor 1]
   - Why they're a competitor
   - Market overlap
   
2. [Competitor 2]
3. [Competitor 3]

### Aspirational Competitors
- Long-term targets

## Keyword Analysis
### Competitor Keyword Summary
| Competitor | Total Keywords | Top 10 Keywords | Est. Traffic |
|------------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Comp 1     | 5,847        | 234            | 125,000      |
| Comp 2     | 4,234        | 189            | 98,000       |

### Keyword Opportunities
Top 20 keywords we should target (with difficulty scores)

### Keyword Gaps
Keywords competitors rank for, we don't (prioritized list)

## Content Analysis
### Content Type Performance
- What content formats work best in your niche
- Average word counts for top-ranking content
- Multimedia usage patterns

### Content Gaps
- Topics competitors cover that you don't
- Content depth comparison
- Update frequency analysis

## Backlink Analysis
### Competitor Link Profiles
| Competitor | Backlinks | Referring Domains | DR |
|------------|-----------|-------------------|-----|
| Comp 1     | 45,678   | 3,456            | 71  |

### Link Building Opportunities
- High-quality domains linking to competitors
- Broken link opportunities
- Resource page opportunities

## Technical SEO Comparison
- Site speed comparison
- Mobile optimization status
- Schema markup usage
- Site architecture quality

## Strategic Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (0-30 days)
1. [Action] - Expected Impact: [%]
2. [Action] - Expected Impact: [%]

### Short-term Actions (1-3 months)
1. [Action] - Expected Impact: [%]

### Long-term Strategy (3-12 months)
1. [Action] - Expected Impact: [%]

## Resource Requirements
- Budget needed
- Team time investment
- Tools required

## Success Metrics
- KPIs to track
- Expected timeline for results
- Benchmark goals

## Appendix
- Detailed data tables
- Tool screenshots
- Methodology notes
```

```
# Competitive Analysis Report: GreenThumbGarden.com

Date: January 15, 2025
Prepared by: SEO Team

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines our competitive landscape in the home gardening niche. 
We identified 5 direct competitors and analyzed 50,000+ keywords, 10,000+ 
backlinks, and 500+ pieces of content.

KEY FINDINGS:
• We're missing 1,847 high-value keyword opportunities
• Competitors have 3.2x more referring domains on average
• Our content is 42% shorter than top-ranking competitors
• Technical SEO gaps in site speed and schema markup

TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS:
1. Target 20 high-priority keywords (Est. +15,000 monthly visitors)
2. Implement comprehensive schema markup (Est. +25% CTR)
3. Create 12 ultimate guides to fill content gaps (Est. +35 backlinks)

EXPECTED IMPACT:
• 6 months: +120% organic traffic
• 12 months: +280% organic traffic
• New ranking keywords: 500+

## Strategic Recommendations

### Phase 1: Immediate Actions (0-30 days)

**1. Fix Critical Technical Issues**
- Improve site speed (currently 4.2s, target 1.5s)
- Implement schema markup (Article, HowTo, FAQPage)

- Action: Development team, 1 week
- Expected Impact: +15% CTR, +10% traffic

- Average difficulty: 18
- Combined search volume: 28,900/month
- Action: Content team, 3 weeks
- Expected Impact: +25,000 monthly visitors

**3. Launch Link Building Campaign**
- Submit to 15 high-DR directories
- Reach out to 30 resource pages
- Action: SEO team, ongoing
- Expected Impact: +45 referring domains

### Phase 2: Short-term (1-3 months)

**1. Create 12 Ultimate Guides**
- Topics from keyword gap analysis
- Minimum 5,000 words each
- Include videos, infographics, downloadables
- Action: Content team, 12 weeks
- Expected Impact: +150,000 monthly visitors, +67 backlinks

**2. Video Content Series**
- 24 how-to videos on top topics
- Embed in existing content
- Upload to YouTube for additional traffic
- Action: Video team, 10 weeks
- Expected Impact: +35% engagement, +50,000 YouTube views/month

**3. Interactive Tools Development**
- Planting calendar tool
- Garden planner calculator
- Companion planting matcher
- Action: Development team, 8 weeks
- Expected Impact: +89 backlinks, +15% conversion

### Phase 3: Long-term (3-12 months)

**1. Content Expansion**
- 100+ new in-depth articles
- Update all existing content
- Add multimedia to top 50 pages
- Action: Content team, ongoing
- Expected Impact: +500 ranking keywords

**2. Authority Building**
- Create scholarship program (+12 .edu links)
- Launch original research/survey (industry data)
- Expert contributor program
- Action: Marketing team, ongoing
- Expected Impact: +200 referring domains, DR increase to 65

**3. Site Restructure**
- Reduce click depth from 6 to 3

- Optimize internal linking (4 to 12 links/page)
- Action: SEO + Development team, 6 weeks
- Expected Impact: +40% indexation, +25% traffic
```
