**Objective:** Understand which SERP features appear for your keywords and capture them.

**SERP Features to Track:**


### 3. User Intent Mapping

**Definition:** Understanding the true intent behind search queries to deliver the right content.

**Intent Categories:**

```
Search Intent Classification

Informational Intent:
├── Learning/education
├── Research/understanding
├── Problem diagnosis
└── General knowledge

Examples:
- "what is [topic]"
- "how does [thing] work"
- "why is [phenomenon]"
- "difference between X and Y"

Commercial Investigation Intent:
├── Product research
├── Comparison shopping
├── Review reading
└── Solution evaluation

Examples:
- "best [product]"
- "X vs Y"
- "[product] review"
- "top [category]"

Transactional Intent:
├── Ready to purchase
├── Ready to sign up
├── Ready to book
└── Ready to download

Examples:
- "buy [product]"
- "[service] near me"
- "[product] price"
- "sign up for [service]"

Navigational Intent:
├── Finding specific site
├── Looking for brand
├── Accessing tool/feature
└── Login/account access

Examples:
- "[brand name]"
- "[brand] login"
- "[brand] support"
- "[specific page] [brand]"
```

### 4. Ranking Volatility Monitoring

**Definition:** Tracking how stable your rankings are and identifying risks.

**Volatility Metrics:**

```
Volatility Measurement Framework

Position Volatility:
├── Daily rank tracking
├── Calculate standard deviation
├── Identify unusual fluctuations
└── Alert on significant changes

Score Calculation:
Volatility Score = Standard Deviation of Positions (30 days)
- Low volatility: 0-2 positions
- Medium volatility: 2-5 positions
- High volatility: 5+ positions

Traffic Volatility:
├── Daily organic traffic tracking
├── Compare to expected baseline
├── Identify anomalies
└── Correlate with rank changes

Algorithm Update Impact:
├── Monitor industry volatility indicators
├── Compare your changes to industry
├── Identify affected pages
└── Assess cause
```
